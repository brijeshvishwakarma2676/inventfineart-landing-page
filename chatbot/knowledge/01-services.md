# Services

Invent Fine Art lists seven services. The descriptions below are the studio's own wording (lightly tidied for spelling). The website does **not** give detailed materials, sizes, finishes or applications for these services. Do not add any.

Related website pages: /services (all services) and the gallery pages named below.

## 01. Wall Murals
"Great material, great selection, great value." The studio says that when it comes to selection, no one offers more murals and unique mural art work, for decorating a home or an office. It also offers "Mural Walls". Gallery: /gallery/wall-murals

## 02. Gate Grills
"Our product range is designed and fabricated using supreme quality material." The studio says the designs, colours and patterns of the product add charm to the architectural design of a house, office and other places. Grills are available in numerous designs, specifications and customisations. Customised GRC grills, railings, pergolas and gazebos are among the work it executes. Gallery: /gallery/grc-products

## 03. Artificial Rockery
"Rockery Art satisfies people's desire to return to Nature by offering them stone fragments from Nature." (Rockery work is also listed among the art installations the studio executes.) Related gallery: /gallery/other

## 04. Water Fountains
"Totally unique, fountain technology specialising in only fountains and sculptural water features." The studio plans them, designs them, builds the custom vaults, constructs them, maintains them and services them. Gallery: /gallery/water-fountains

## 05. Sculptures Art Installation
Indoor and outdoor sculpture for home and garden decoration. The studio says the presence of sculpture can make you relax, bring more fun to your life and help maintain a more beautiful home and garden. Its sculptures are suitable for home, park, villa, hotels and garden. Gallery: /gallery/sculptures

## 06. Architectural Facades
"We create a complete architectural façade with modern and traditional designs for our clients." The studio also undertakes a labour contract for architectural façades, and says it brings together all the elements to create architectural wall and roof facades to suit a building's designs and specifications. (3D wall facades are also listed among the art installations it executes.) Gallery: /gallery/grc-products

## 07. Planters
Indoor and outdoor planters are described as a perfect way to create beautiful container gardens for a front porch, patio and any indoor or outdoor space. The website states that **planters and garden pots are made in GRC and FRP material**. Gallery: /gallery/planters

## Materials: what the website actually says
Only two materials are named: **GRC** (for planters, and for customised grills, railings, pergolas and gazebos) and **FRP** (for planters). The website does not list other materials or finishes. For anything else, direct the visitor to contact the studio.
