# Common questions

**What does Invent Fine Art do?** It is an ISO 9001 : 2008 certified company engaged in trading, manufacturing, supplying and fixing art work for indoor and outdoor decor, including sculptures, murals, water fountains, architectural facades, planters, gate grills and rockery work. (See identity and services.)

**Is it ISO certified?** Yes, the studio states it is ISO 9001 : 2008 certified.

**Can I get something made to my own design?** Yes. The studio says it makes art products as per design and requirement, and offers products in numerous sizes, specifications and dimensions. It provides start to end solutions from conceptualisation to complete execution.

**Do you install the work?** The studio describes itself as engaged in supplying and fixing art work, and for facades it also undertakes labour contracts. Products are delivered in premium packing. For details of installation for a specific project, the visitor should contact the studio.

**What materials do you use?** The website only names GRC and FRP (planters and garden pots are made in GRC and FRP; GRC is used for customised grills, railings, pergolas and gazebos). It describes materials otherwise only as "top class raw material". For other materials, contact the studio.

**Are the products suitable for outdoors?** The studio makes indoor and outdoor decor products, indoor and outdoor sculpture and planters, and fountains. The website does not publish weather-resistance or durability ratings; it only says the products have a seamless finish, durability and longer life. For project-specific advice, contact the studio.

**Can you handle urgent or bulk orders?** The studio says it can meet urgent and bulk orders within the committed time. It does not publish specific timelines.

**Do you do turnkey projects?** Yes, the studio says it can do turnkey projects.

**Do you work outside Mumbai or abroad?** The studio describes itself as supplying and exporting, and says its customers are "present all across the globe". The visitor should contact the studio with their location.

**Where are you based?** Office in Kandivali East, Mumbai; factory opposite Jag Mata Mandir, Wagholi Road, Nalasopra West, Thane. Full addresses are in the contact section.

**How do I get a quote or start a project?** Use the Contact page form, call or message the listed numbers, or email. From a gallery lightbox, "Request a quote for this piece" fills in the piece reference.

**What should I include in an enquiry?** Helpful details: what you need (sculpture, mural, fountain, facade, planter, grill, rockery), the location, approximate dimensions, indoor or outdoor placement, and your timeline.
