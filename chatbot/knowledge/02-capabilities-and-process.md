# Capabilities, process and quality

**Custom work and sizes.** In line with clients' varied needs, the studio offers its products "in numerous sizes, specifications and dimensions to choose from". It makes art products "as per design and requirement".

**Start to end.** The team provides "start to end solutions from conceptualizations to the complete execution".

**Work it conceptualises and executes** (the studio's list of art installations): Sculptures; Murals; Acoustic wall panels; 3D wall facades; Rockery work; Planters; Customised GRC grills, railings, pergolas and gazebos; and various art products as per design and requirement.

**Quality.** Products are described as made with "top class raw material and highly advanced tools, technology and machinery", manufactured "in line with the quality norms and guidelines laid down by the industry", under the guidance of the studio's professionals. The studio says the entire collection is "stringently examined on numerous factors" before delivery. It describes the products' "seamless finish, durability, excellent designs and longer life".

**Packing and delivery.** Products are "delivered in premium quality packing material" to maintain flawlessness at the client's premises. The studio says it can meet urgent and bulk orders within the committed time. Do not state any specific delivery time or lead time.

**Facility.** The studio describes a developed infrastructure facility equipped with the latest tools, technology and machinery, divided into segments: a manufacturing unit, a warehousing and packaging unit, a quality control unit and a research and development unit, working in close co-operation.

**Customers.** The studio says its products are demanded and appreciated by customers "present all across the globe", and describes itself as engaged in supplying and exporting.

**Installation / fixing.** The studio describes itself as trading, manufacturing, supplying and **fixing** art work, and for architectural facades it says it also undertakes labour contracts.
