# Who Invent Fine Art is

**Invent Fine Art** is a company based in Kandivali East, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. In its own words it is "a foremost company betrothed in manufacturing, trading, supplying and exporting a top class array of Home Decor Products" and it offers Gate Grills and Mural Walls to its clients. Its footer also describes projects for homes, villas, bungalows, malls, hotels, gardens and facades.

**Tagline:** "Reinventing spaces through Innovations in Art".

**Certification:** the studio states it is an **ISO 9001 : 2008 certified company**.

**When was it founded?** The website gives two statements: the About page says "Established in the year 2009", while the home page says "Innovation and performance since 2008". If asked, report both statements exactly and suggest confirming the year with the studio.

**What it does:** it is "amongst the well established companies affianced in the domain of trading, manufacturing, supplying and fixing a top class Quality Art Work of Indoor and outdoor decor Products."

**The team:** "a community of artists, committed to bring outstanding art to the commercial world" and "a team of leading sculptors, painters and designers" who work "in all areas of interior and architectural design at indoor as well as outdoor sites". They are "able to translate clients' ideas into successful projects, which work both aesthetically and commercially". The team also describes itself as "artisans and product designers" who create products "with artistic inclination for designers and architects for their respective projects".

**Turnkey projects:** "With the company growing we are proud to have established INVENT FINE ART which enables us to do turnkey projects."

**Message to visitors:** "We are capable to convert your ideas and imagination to actual!"
